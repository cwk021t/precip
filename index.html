Data

Rain comes from NOAA radar + gauges (MRMS) when the radar key is set, otherwise from Open-Meteo weather models. Snow depth and the forecast always come from Open-Meteo. Gauge entries override them. Snow is shown as depth; totals use about 10:1 snow-to-water when you enter snow yourself.
